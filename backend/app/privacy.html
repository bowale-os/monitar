Monitar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 June 2026

Monitar is a browser extension whose single purpose is to let you save your open browser tabs as a session and restore them later. This policy explains what data Monitar collects, why, and how it is handled.

Data we collect

  • Account information — your name and email address, and a password which is stored only as a one-way hash (we never store your plaintext password).
  • Session data — when you explicitly start a session, we record the URLs and page titles of your tabs, the time each tab was open, and the order in which tabs were opened. This data is collected only while a session is active.
  • An optional "intent" — any text you choose to type to describe what a session is for.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect the contents of the web pages you visit (text, images, form inputs, etc.).
  • We do not collect location, financial, or health information.
  • Monitar does not track your browsing outside of sessions you start, and injects no scripts into third-party websites.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide Monitar's features: to authenticate you, and to save, list, restore, and delete your sessions. We do not use it for any unrelated purpose.

How your data is stored

  • Sessions and account details are stored in your account on Monitar's backend database, transmitted over HTTPS.
  • Authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local) so you stay signed in. These are cleared when you sign out.

Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties. We do not use or transfer your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. Your data is only ever sent to Monitar's own backend service.

Data retention and deletion

  • You can delete any saved session at any time from within the extension.
  • Signing out clears all Monitar data stored in your browser.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below.
