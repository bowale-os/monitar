Monitar

Never lose your train of thought again.

You close 40 tabs at the end of a deep research session, and by tomorrow you can't remember why half of them mattered. Monitar remembers for you.

Start a session, browse like you normally do, and Monitar quietly saves the full context — every tab, every group — so you can pick it back up in seconds, days or weeks later.

How it works

  1. Hit Start when you begin a task — "Q3 budget research", "apartment hunting", whatever it is.
  2. Browse however you want — nothing to remember, nothing to manage.
  3. Stop, and it's saved. Find it again in one search, weeks from now, by typing what you were doing — not the exact title or URL.

That last part is the whole point: searching by what you were doing, not what you called it.

Your browsing stays yours.

Session content is encrypted on your device before it ever leaves your browser — Monitar's servers never see your tabs in plaintext.

  • Sees your open tabs only while a session is running.
  • Remembers tab groups so restored sessions look the way you left them.
  • Runs a save timer locally — no constant background tracking.

One-time setup, then it's instant.

The first time you search, Monitar downloads a small on-device search model — a few seconds, once, cached after that. Everything after is instant, and nothing about your search ever leaves your device.
