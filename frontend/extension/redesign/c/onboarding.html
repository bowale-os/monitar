You close 40 tabs at the end of a deep-research night, and by next week you can't remember why half of them mattered. Monitar remembers for you — and brings it all back.

Private by architecture, not by promise.

  • Encrypted before it leaves your device. Your sessions are sealed with a key made from your password. Our servers store ciphertext — we can't read your tabs.
  • Search never phones home. The search model runs inside your browser. Your queries never leave your device.
  • Tracking only when you say so. Monitar sees tabs only while a session you started is running. No history access, no page contents.

One account. Zero readable data.

Your account only syncs encrypted sessions across your devices. Your password doubles as your encryption key — it never leaves your browser.

Because we never see your password, we can't recover your sessions if you forget it. Use a password manager.
